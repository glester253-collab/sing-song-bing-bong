# sing-song-bing-bong

## Project Brief

A voice-driven West Coast hip-hop music production project with the following specifications:

### Core Concept
- **Voice-First Creation**: Create a song using my voice as the primary instrument
- **On-Beat Sync**: Ensure vocals are synced and on-beat throughout
- **Wordplay & Articulation**: Emphasis on rhyme expertise, wordplay, and clear vocal articulation

### Production Style
- **Inspirations**: West Coast hip-hop influence (Nipsey Hussle, 2Pac styles)
- **Production Tools**: Beat machine, voice synthesizer, and state-of-the-art studio equipment
- **Quality Standards**: Studio-grade digital tracks with professional mix and mastering

### Talent & Support
- **Production Team**: Expert music producer skilled in all instruments
- **Vocal Coaching**: Professional vocal coaches to enhance delivery
- **Artist Reference**: Best-of-the-best vocal quality standards from top rappers

### Expected Output
A professionally produced hip-hop track featuring my voice as the lead vocal, produced at the highest industry standards.
